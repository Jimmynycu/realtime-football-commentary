The production line

A demo is one finished thing. A system is the line that makes it, checks it and improves it, so the next thing costs less than the first. Six parts; each reports its own state.

What comes off the line

Products built on the same data, the same checks and the same harness. The live commentary is the first; the others reuse what it needed.

Asset library

One search over every line on air, every preparation note and every highlight. Accents and case do not matter.

Rebuild

The lines on air are computed from everything below. Change an input, then rebuild; the next viewer to connect gets the new lines. The two long jobs run in the background and rebuild when they finish.

Sources

Every input and every derived file: what it is for, where it comes from, how fresh it is, and whether it leaves this machine.
