# Argentina beat France 4-2 on penalties after a 3-3 draw in the World Cup final

*FIFA World Cup 2022 Final. Argentina 3-3 France after extra time; Argentina 4-2 on penalties. Top scorer: Mbappé, with a hat-trick.*

With the score still 3-3 after extra time, the shootout settled it, and Argentina won it 4-2. By the numbers, Argentina had 54% of the ball and 20 shots (2.76 xG) against France's 10 (2.27 xG).
